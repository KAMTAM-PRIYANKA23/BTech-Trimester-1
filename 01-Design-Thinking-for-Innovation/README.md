# Design Thinking for Innovation

During Trimester 1, I was introduced to design thinking and how it can be used to approach real-world problems through ideas, planning and early-stage design.

## Concept: Tourism at Hidden Places

As part of this subject, I worked on a concept called **Tourism at Hidden Places**, focused on bringing attention to lesser-known tourist destinations.

### My Work

- Identified the problem and explored the idea behind the concept.
- Thought about how people could discover places that are less commonly known.
- Sketched the proposed frontend layout on paper.
- Created a paper-based prototype to communicate the proposed design.
- Planned how the concept could move towards implementation.

### What I Learned

This experience helped me understand that developing a solution starts with understanding the problem. I learned to think about the user's perspective, organise an idea and represent it visually before moving towards development.

The project gave me an introduction to the early stages of product thinking, from identifying a problem to presenting a possible solution.
