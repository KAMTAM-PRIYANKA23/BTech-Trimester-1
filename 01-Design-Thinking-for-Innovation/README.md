# Design Thinking for Innovation

As part of my first trimester, I studied how design thinking can be used to understand problems and develop ideas around real-world needs.

## Concept Work: Tourism at Hidden Places

One of the concepts I worked on was **Tourism at Hidden Places**. The idea focused on exploring ways to bring attention to lesser-known tourist destinations.

### Work I Completed

- Studied the problem and worked on identifying the need behind the concept.
- Developed the initial idea around promoting lesser-known places.
- Sketched possible frontend screens and layouts on paper.
- Created a paper-based prototype to represent how the idea could look.
- Considered how the concept could be taken forward into implementation.

### What I Learned

This work gave me an opportunity to approach a problem before thinking about its technical implementation. I learned to break an idea into smaller parts, think about how a user might interact with it, and represent an early design through sketches and a prototype.

### Project Status

The work documented here represents the concept development and paper-prototyping stage. It was not a completed coded or deployed application.
