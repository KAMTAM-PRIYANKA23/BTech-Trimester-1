# Design Thinking for Innovation

## Overview

Design Thinking for Innovation introduced a human-centred approach to understanding problems and developing possible solutions. The coursework focused on understanding the problem before deciding on a solution, exploring ideas and representing concepts through early prototypes.

## Concept: Tourism at Hidden Places

The proposed concept, **Tourism at Hidden Places**, explored the idea of helping people discover lesser-known tourist destinations.

The work focused on the early design stages of a possible digital solution rather than building a finished application.

## Work Undertaken

### 1. Understanding the Problem
- Studied the fundamentals and purpose of Design Thinking.
- Practised identifying problems from given situations.
- Worked on framing and defining a clear problem statement.

### 2. Idea Development
- Explored the Tourism at Hidden Places concept.
- Considered the problem the proposed idea aimed to address.
- Discussed how a digital solution could support the concept.

### 3. Paper-Based Prototyping
- Sketched possible frontend screens on paper and charts.
- Planned the basic appearance and flow of the proposed interface.
- Used sketches to communicate the idea before implementation.

### 4. Implementation Planning
- Studied the role of prototypes in the design process.
- Planned how the proposed concept could be developed further.

## Learning Outcomes

- Understanding a problem before proposing a solution.
- Breaking a broad problem into a clearer problem statement.
- Exploring and communicating ideas through sketches.
- Understanding the purpose of prototypes in early product design.
- Connecting design concepts with possible real-world applications.

## Project Status

**Scope:** Concept development and paper-based prototyping.

The work documented here represents the design and planning stage. It is not a completed coded, tested or deployed application.
